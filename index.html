Rao's Homemade — "Tommy Cutlets"

In late 2023, a narrow window of opportunity emerged around Tommy DeVito's viral moment as the Italian-American QB lighting up the New York Giants' late-season run. The partnership between Tommy and Rao's Homemade was brokered and executed quickly, leaning fully into his cultural identity, filming an intimate shoot at the DeVito family home in New Jersey with Tommy and his mother Lexi, building a custom "Tommy Cutlets" recipe hosted on Rao's.com, and securing three PR opportunities for the brand. The result: $245,000 in validated brand exposure value with coverage in the New York Post, Bleacher Report, and USA Today.

NIL Campaign — U.S. Marine Corps

"Lead Like a Marine"

Partnered with the United States Marine Corps to execute the "Lead Like a Marine" initiative, an aspirational brand campaign targeting prospect-aged youth. Directed and produced a content suite featuring a University of Tennessee student-athlete, shifting the narrative away from standard military recruitment toward a focused conversation on elite leadership, discipline, and personal growth.

Strategy & Execution

Niche Integration

Selected core Marine Corps leadership traits that mirrored the high-performance demands of a Division I student-athlete, creating a natural crossover for the target audience.

Multi-Phase Rollout

Directed a multi-platform digital cadence starting with an interactive Instagram Q&A teaser, followed by long-form video content, and concluding with a reflective recap post.

Activity-Based Feature

Produced a long-form interview anchored by an in-person training session, translating military values into relatable stories about athletic grit and community leadership.
